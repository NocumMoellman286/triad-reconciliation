Third-party components and data sources
=======================================

No third-party data is redistributed by this release. The analysed cohort is private and no
record from it ships. The auxiliary corpora below are named by the manuscript and are used
only for representation pretraining; each remains governed by its own terms, and no image or
table from any of them is included here.

Datasets named by the manuscript
--------------------------------

PAPILA
  Source    : https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/PAPILA/14798004
  Descriptor: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01388-1
  Licence   : GPL 3.0+ as stated on the deposition page
  Note      : the descriptor asks to be cited rather than the deposition

ACRIMA labelled fundus images
  Source    : https://doi.org/10.1186/s12938-019-0649-y
  Licence   : distributed for research use; no separate licence statement on the article page

RIM-ONE DL
  Source    : https://github.com/miag-ull/rim-one-dl
  Licence   : research and educational use, free of charge; redistribution and commercial use
              prohibited, as stated in the repository README

ODIR-5K
  Source    : https://odir2019.grand-challenge.org/
  Licence   : challenge terms as specified on the challenge page
